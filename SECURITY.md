# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Ironclad takes security seriously. If you believe you have found a security
vulnerability in Ironclad, please report it to us **privately** before
disclosing it publicly.

**Contact:** `security@ironclad.dev` (placeholder — replace with the real
address before launch)

Please include:

- The affected component (package, file, endpoint).
- A description of the vulnerability and its impact.
- Steps to reproduce, including a minimal proof of concept if possible.
- Your contact information for follow-up.

## Response SLA

- **Ack:** we will acknowledge receipt within **2 business days**.
- **Triage:** you will receive an initial assessment (confirmed / not
  confirmed / out of scope) within **5 business days**.
- **Fix:** for confirmed issues, we aim to ship a fix or mitigation within
  **30 days**, coordinated with you.

## Disclosure policy

- **No public disclosure before a fix is available.** We coordinate disclosure
  with reporters and give credit where credit is due (unless you prefer to
  remain anonymous).
- We ask reporters to give us a reasonable window (default 90 days) to fix
  and roll out a fix before public disclosure.
